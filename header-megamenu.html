Shop by category

Prototype: popular categories with a nested dropdown

  • Mobile (≤760 px) uses the Walmart pattern. The category ribbon is replaced by a Shop by category button, and the grid icon left of the search opens the same thing. It opens a bottom sheet with an accordion of the header categories. A ⌄ category expands in place to show its subgroups as plain links, plus "View all …". Only one category is open at a time. A category without subgroups is a plain › link. Child categories are desktop-only, the same as on Walmart mobile.
  • Desktop: hover a header category to open the full-width panel. Bold lines are subgroups and white lines under them are child categories. A subgroup without children shows as a bold line only, like "Clutches" in the Walmart menu.
  • The top-level items match the current autodoc.co.uk header, including the Trending and New labels. The dropdown content is placeholder data: each link carries a dashed stub tag.
  • Behaviour: opens after 120 ms and closes 200 ms after the cursor leaves, so moving the mouse diagonally doesn't flicker the panel. The page behind is dimmed. Esc closes the panel. Arrow keys move between header items.
  • All data sits in one MENU array in the script: a top item, its subgroups, then their children. The order of the array is the display order. The panel has up to 6 columns. Subgroups are split across them automatically by line count, keeping their order.
